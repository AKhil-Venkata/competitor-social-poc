Privacy Policy

Social Media Competitor Analytics POC

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Purpose

This is an internal proof-of-concept tool used to evaluate public social-media content and engagement information for competitor benchmarking and analytics.

Information accessed

The tool may access publicly available social-media profile, post, video, caption, timestamp, and engagement information through platform APIs, subject to platform permissions and availability. For account authentication and access to assets managed by an authorized user, it may process access tokens and account or Page identifiers.

Use of information

Information is used only for internal testing, analytics, benchmarking, reporting, and evaluation of this proof of concept. The tool does not sell information or use it for advertising targeting.

Storage and sharing

During this proof of concept, data is stored locally in files on the operator's computer or approved local storage. Data is not intentionally shared with third parties through this tool, except as required by the relevant platform APIs or applicable law. Access tokens and credentials should be stored securely and not included in reports or shared files.

Retention and deletion

Data may be retained for the duration of internal testing. The operator can delete locally stored data and credentials when they are no longer needed.

Platform terms

Use of Facebook, Instagram, and other social-media APIs is subject to each platform's terms, policies, permissions, and technical limitations. This tool only accesses data permitted by those rules and granted access.

Contact

For privacy questions about this internal proof of concept, contact the internal project owner or administrator.

This policy is a draft for an internal proof of concept and should be reviewed and approved by the organisation before public use or submission to platform review.
